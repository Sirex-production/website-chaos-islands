WHAT WE COLLECT

Only if you turn the toggles on. Nothing is collected if you decline.

Error reports:

  • Error or crash message and stack trace
  • The part of the game the error happened in
  • Game version

Difficulty reports:

  • When you start a new game
  • Tutorial steps you complete
  • Comic pages you view
  • For each session: how long it lasted, whether you won or lost, turns played, quests completed, enemies defeated
  • Chapters you finish and how they ended
  • Whether you reached the end of the demo
  • Game version

Collected automatically by Unity in both cases:

  • Device identifier
  • Device model and operating system
  • IP address
  • Session ID

We do not collect your name, email, or account details.

WHY

To fix crashes and balance difficulty. Nothing else.

LEGAL BASIS

Your consent. You can withdraw it any time in Settings, which stops collection immediately. Data collected before that stays until deletion (below).

WHO ELSE SEES IT

Unity Technologies processes this data on our behalf, on servers in the United States:
https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
We do not sell it, share it with advertisers, or use it for ads.

HOW LONG WE KEEP IT

All demo data is deleted when Chaos Islands releases.

YOUR RIGHTS

The data is not linked to your name or email, so we cannot tell which records are yours. If you want your data deleted or shown to you, write to sirexproductions@gmail.com and we will help as far as we are able to identify the records.

You can stop collection any time by turning the toggles off in Settings > Game.

You can also complain to the Polish data protection authority (UODO, uodo.gov.pl).

CHANGES

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and note it on our website.
